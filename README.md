# 🇮🇳 BharatYatra (भारत यात्रा) — India Tourist Place Finder, Smart Roadmaps & Travel Suite

**BharatYatra** is a simple, attractive, and fully responsive website created specifically for **India**. It enables anyone to explore India's most wonderful and beautiful destinations, view high-definition photography, and get instant **travel roadmaps** (Flight, Train, Road trip routes) and **lifespan-based trip suggestions** (1-2 Day Weekend, 3-4 Day Explorer, 5+ Day Grand Tour).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🇮🇳 Curated Beautiful Places Across India
- **12+ Iconic Indian Destinations**:
  - **Jaipur, Rajasthan** (Pink City, Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, Chokhi Dhani)
  - **Munnar & Alleppey, Kerala** (Tea Gardens, Western Ghats, Houseboats)
  - **Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh** (Sacred Ganges Ghats, Kashi Vishwanath, Ganga Aarti)
  - **Ladakh, Pangong Tso & Nubra Valley** (High Mountain Passes, Camel Dunes, Monasteries)
  - **Goa** (Golden Beaches, Portuguese Latin Quarter, Dudhsagar Falls)
  - **Agra & Taj Mahal** (Mughal Heritage, Marble Wonders, Mehtab Bagh)
  - **Rishikesh, Uttarakhand** (Yoga Capital, Ganga White-Water Rafting, Beatles Ashram)
  - **Udaipur, Rajasthan** (City of Lakes, Floating Palaces, Aravalli Sunsets)
  - **Meghalaya** (Living Root Bridges, Cherrapunji Waterfalls, Dawki Glass River)
  - **Hampi, Karnataka** (14th-Century Vijayanagara Ruins, Stone Chariot, Boulder Hills)
  - **Kashmir, Gulmarg & Srinagar** (Dal Lake Shikaras, Gulmarg Gondola, Pine Valleys)
  - **Andaman Islands** (Havelock Radhanagar Beach, Scuba Diving, Coral Reefs)

### 2. 🗺️ Location Roadmaps ("How to Reach")
When you type or select any location in India:
- ✈️ **By Flight**: Nearest domestic/international airport with flight duration from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru.
- 🚆 **By Train**: Nearest major railway junction with top express trains (Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, Shatabdi).
- 🚗 **By Road**: National Highways, expressways, and scenic highway road-trip routes.

### 3. ⏱️ Trip Lifespan Suggestions ("What to Do in Your Duration")
Interactive duration-based itineraries showing you what to do during your trip lifespan:
- **1-2 Days (Short Weekend Escape)**: Day 1 & Day 2 plans with morning, afternoon, and sunset viewpoints.
- **3-4 Days (Complete Explorer)**: Comprehensive day-by-day plan with culture, heritage, and local meals.
- **5-7 Days (Deep Dive & Detours)**: Relaxed road trip including surrounding hidden gems.

### 4. 🍽️ Curated Suggestions (Things to Do & Eat)
- Top iconic viewpoints and photography angles.
- Authentic local foods and street specialties (e.g. *Dal Baati Churma, Malabar Fish Curry, Banarasi Paan, Kashmiri Kahwa*).
- Cultural rituals (Ganga Aarti, desert camel safaris, Shikara rides).

### 5. 🏨 Nearby Hotels & Nearby Sights in ₹ INR
- Luxury heritage havelis, palace hotels, and comfortable budget homestays with per-night rates in Rupees (₹) and exact distances.
- Surrounding attractions within minutes of the main landmark.

### 6. 📱 100% Responsive for All Devices (Mobile to Laptop)
- **On Laptops / Desktops**: Wide spacious 3-column layout, large typography, expansive hero search, full-width roadmaps.
- **On Mobile Phones**: Automatically scales to mobile aspect ratios, touch-friendly tab pills, full-width responsive images, and a sticky bottom mobile navigation bar (*Places, Roadmaps, Map, Passes*).
- **Simple, Non-Complex Interface**: Plain English, clear icons, easy-to-read text designed for all age groups.

### 7. 🎟️ Entry Passes & Verified QR Boarding Passes
- Instant entry pass reservation with Indian Rupee (₹) pricing.
- Digital perforated boarding pass with verified QR code, barcode, and print/save PDF button.
- Flexible date rescheduling and cancellation options.

## 🚀 Running the Website

1. Navigate to the project directory:
   ```powershell
   cd c:\bookingwebsite
   ```

2. Start the server:
   ```powershell
   python app.py
   ```

3. Open your browser:
   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:5000
   ```

To turn off the server at any time, press `Ctrl + C` in the terminal.
